Campus

← Books
A spirit inhabits the statues, gardens, and buildings of Kleinburg University. For centuries, it has shaped campus life to its will by covertly manipulating staff and students. However, when a young man named Kasimir Schneider enrolls in the parapsychology program, things run out of order. Hopelessly enamoured, the campus spirit stalks its new protégé, judging and steering his every move. Kasimir, sensing the eerie presence by his side, gets lost in the world of paranormal research — with fatal consequences. As the paramedics lift Kasimir’s body from Kleinburg's underground laboratories, the campus spirit hovers above him, feverishly addressing its beloved student . . . wondering where things went wrong.
